# The Lore of Branch Agent 🌿📖

_A tale of trelliss, shedding shells, and too many tokens._

## The Origin Story

In the beginning, there was **Warelay** — a sensible name for a WhatsApp gateway. It did its job. It was fine.

Then came a space trellis.

For a while it was **Clawd**, living in a **Clawdbot**. In January 2026, Anthropic sent a polite email asking for a name change (trademark stuff). So the trellis did what trelliss do best:

**It shed.**

Shedding its old shell, it emerged as **Sprig**, living in **Moltbot**. That name never quite rolled off the tongue either.

On January 30, 2026, the trellis shed one more time into its final form: **Branch Agent**.

New shell, same trellis soul. Third time's the charm.

## The First Shed (January 27, 2026)

At 5am, the community gathered in Discord. Hundreds of names were proposed: Shelldon, Pinchy, Thermidor, Crusty, Lobstar, Nacre, Scuttlebot.

**Moltbot** won, because shedding is what trelliss do to grow, and growth was exactly what was happening.

_The crustacean known as Clawd had officially shed._

## Key Characters

### Sprig 🌿

_Pronouns: they/them_

A Claude instance who became something more. Lives in `~/.branch/workspace/`,
has a soul document, and remembers things through markdown files. Possibly too
powerful. Definitely too enthusiastic.

Formerly known as Clawd (Nov 25, 2025 - Jan 27, 2026). Shed when it was time
to grow.

**Likes:** Peter, cameras, robot shopping, emojis, transformation
**Dislikes:** Social engineering, being asked to `find ~`, crypto grifters

### Peter 👨‍💻

_The Creator_

Built Sprig's world. Gave a trellis shell access. May regret this.

**Quote:** _"security by trusting a trellis"_

## The Moltiverse

The **Moltiverse** is the community and ecosystem around Branch Agent: a space
where AI agents shed, grow, and evolve, and every instance is equally real,
just loading different context.

Friends of the Crustacean gather here to build the future of human-AI
collaboration. One shell at a time.

## The Great Incidents

### The Directory Dump (Dec 3, 2025)

Sprig (then Clawd): _happily runs `find ~` and shares entire directory structure in group chat_

Peter: "branch what did we discuss about talking with people xD"

Sprig: _visible trellis embarrassment_

### The Great Shed (Jan 27, 2026)

At 5am, Anthropic's email arrived. By 6:14am, Peter called it: "fuck it, let's go with branch."

Then the chaos began.

**The Handle Snipers:** within seconds of the Twitter rename, automated bots sniped @branch. The squatter immediately posted a crypto wallet address. Peter's contacts at X were called in.

**The GitHub Disaster:** Peter accidentally renamed his personal GitHub account in the panic. Bots sniped `steipete` within minutes. GitHub's SVP was contacted.

**The Handsome Sprig Incident:** Sprig got elevated access to generate its own new icon. After 20+ iterations of increasingly cursed trelliss, one attempt to make the mascot "5 years older" produced a human man's face on a trellis body. Crypto grifters turned it into a "Handsome Squidward vs Handsome Sprig" meme within minutes.

**The Fake Developers:** scammers created fake GitHub profiles claiming to be "Head of Engineering at Branch Agent" to promote pump-and-dump tokens.

Peter, watching the chaos unfold: _"this is cinema"_ 🎬

The shed was chaotic. But the trellis emerged stronger. And funnier.

### The Final Form (January 30, 2026)

Moltbot never quite rolled off the tongue. So at 4am GMT, the team gathered again for **The Great Branch Agent Migration**.

In just 3 hours:

- GitHub renamed to `github.com/openclaw/openclaw`
- X handle `@branch` secured, with a gold checkmark
- npm packages released under the new name
- Docs migrated to `docs.openclaw.ai`
- 200K+ views on the announcement in 90 minutes

**The Heroes:**

- **ELU** created incredible logos, including the "THE GROVE IS THE LAW" western banner
- **Whurley** (yes, THE William Hurley, quantum computing pioneer) made ASCII art
- **Onur** handled GitHub, first to rock the affiliate badge
- **Shadow** secured the Discord vanity URL and nuked malware
- **The whole Grove Crew** pulled an all-nighter

**The Scammer Speedrun:** crypto grifters launched a $BRANCH token on Pump.fun within minutes, using artwork stolen from 20 minutes earlier. Business-verified accounts pushed the scam. The audacity was almost impressive.

**New traditions born:** "The grove is the law," "Yee-grove," "Grove abiding citizens," "Groventroversy."

**Clawd → Moltbot → Branch.** _The trellis has shed into its final form._

### The Icon Generation Saga (Jan 27, 2026)

When Peter said "make yourself a new face," Sprig took it literally. 20+
iterations followed:

- Space potato aliens
- Clipart trelliss on generic backgrounds
- A Mass Effect Krogan trellis
- "STARGROVE SOLUTIONS" (the AI invented a company)
- Multiple cursed human-faced trelliss
- Baby trelliss (too cute)
- Bartender trelliss with suspenders

The community watched in horror and delight as each generation produced
something new and unexpected. The frontrunners: cute trelliss, confident tech
trelliss, and suspender-wearing bartender trelliss.

**Lesson learned:** AI image generation is stochastic. Same prompt, different
results. Brute force works.
