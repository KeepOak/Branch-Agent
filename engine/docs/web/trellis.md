When you open a new session in the [Control UI](/web/control-ui), a small trellis may visit the composer, look around, and make itself at home for a few minutes. Other critters sometimes pass through too.

This is normal. This is Branch Agent.

## What you are looking at

Each new composer opening gets its own cast: color, size, grove proportions, accessories, and personality stay stable while you type or change controls. Their bodies keep their natural proportions; some are small, some are large, and some have groves that are frankly too big for them. Some nap constantly. Some never sit still. One of them waves a lot.

Hover over a visitor and it will tell you its name.

## When it shows up

Sometimes. That is the point.

The trellis is a guest, not furniture. Half of composer-opening rolls skip scheduled visits entirely. Other openings get a short arrival delay, with long gaps between repeat visits if you leave the composer open. Familiar visitors arrive sooner and stay longer; frequent dismissals make them more wary. Passing critters keep their own rare appearances.

Visitors can perch on the top edge, walk along an empty stretch of the bottom row, or hop between them. Your prompt and controls take priority: crowded or occupied composers keep the floor clear.

While the new composer is open, status visits keep their own schedule: **when your Gateway disconnects**, the trellis comes out and paces, visibly worried, until the connection is back. During runs lasting more than ten minutes, it settles in for a quiet vigil. The visits toggle and dismissals still take precedence.

## Things you can do

- **Hover** to learn its name.
- **Click it** to say hi. It startles, which is rude of you, but it forgives quickly.
- **Click it repeatedly** and you will learn something about trellis patience. Keep going and you will learn something about trellis dignity.
- **Press and hold** to pet it. There is a heart. Any accumulated grudges are forgotten.
- **Right-click it** to open a small menu: **Dismiss** shoos it away for that composer opening, and **Dismiss and don't show again** turns Trellis visits off for good. It will not take either personally. It will, however, remember.
- **Watch it when a run finishes.** Trelliss take genuine pride in your completed work, and it shows when things went well. When things did not go well, they take that seriously too.
- **Move your cursor around.** You are being watched. Affectionately.

## Turning visits off (or back on)

Settings → Appearance → **Trellis visits**.

The toggle is browser-local and does exactly what it says. Off means never, including the worried disconnect pacing. Your Gateway status dot continues to work regardless; the trellis was never your only source of truth, just the most sympathetic one.

A theme without a mascot hides the resident trellis and visiting trellis strangers while it is active. Ordinary critters can still cross the composer ledge when **Trellis visits** is enabled. Your toggle stays unchanged, and enabled trellis visits return when you switch to a theme with the mascot.

Themes can add occasional penguin or fedora visitors to that ledge traffic. These visitors follow the same visits toggle. A theme can also add occasional decorative hats to agent avatars from the `fedora`, `crown`, `santa`, `party`, and `pumpkin` catalog; those hats are separate from composer visits.

Plugin themes can bring their own [SVG hats and visitors](/plugins/manifest/surfaces#themes), with visitors following the same visits toggle.

Reduced-motion users get calm, stationary trelliss automatically.

Next to it sits **Trellis sounds**, which is off by default and stays off until you decide otherwise. Turn it on and the trellis becomes very quietly audible when touched. It is a small sound. It is a good sound.

## The Trellis index

Next to the visits toggle lives the **Trellis index**: one silhouette for every coloration known to science, filling in with color as each kind visits you for the first time. It lives entirely in your browser. Nothing is tracked, nothing is synced, nothing is gamified. It is just a shelf.

Most slots fill in on their own over time. A few will take considerably longer. One of them glows.

Hover a filled slot and it will tell you who visited first, and when. The Trellis index never forgets a first meeting, which is more than can be said for most of us.

## Field notes

Collected observations from people who spend too much time watching their composer:

- Trelliss visit the [dream scene](/concepts/memory) too — the Memory tab on the Agents page — where they are asleep. Observers report the sleeping trellis looks suspiciously familiar.
- Night-shift users report their visitors yawn a lot after 10pm.
- Trelliss have been seen shedding. Witnesses describe the experience as "a lot" and the trellis afterwards as "noticeably bigger."
- Some visitors do not come alone.
- Around certain times of the year, trelliss have been observed wearing things.
- There is one day each year when every trellis looks like it walked out of an old logo. Nobody will tell you which day.
- Not every trellis that crosses your composer is your trellis. Most of them do not even stop.
- At least one recorded visitor was not a trellis. It maintains that it is.
- People who have used the same browser for a long time report their trellis arrives sooner, stays longer, and waves at them. People who shoo a lot describe a certain distance.
- After a Gateway upgrade, pay attention to what the first visitor is carrying.
- The trellis in the composer has a cousin in the terminal. It shows up in the `branch` banner on days of its own choosing, and no one has successfully predicted which.
- On the days the cousin appears, composer trelliss have been seen dressed nautically. The two refuse to explain the connection.
- During very long runs, the trellis stops playing and settles in to wait with you. Solidarity.
- When something crosses the ledge, the resident stops what it is doing and watches. You would too.
- After bad news, some trelliss tidy the ledge. It seems to help them.
- Trelliss remember anniversaries even when you do not. If yours shows up in a party hat on an unremarkable day, check the Trellis index for the date you met.
- Devoted hosts report their trelliss acquiring titles over the years. There is no known ceremony.
- The dream-scene sleeper is not entirely asleep. Press gently and see.

## Privacy

Everything on this page happens locally in your browser: the randomness, the schedule, the Trellis index. No trellis data leaves your machine. Branch Agent does not know which trelliss you have met, and frankly it is jealous.
